Paros, Greece

Visit Naousa and explore its architecture and local life. For nightlife, check out Barbarossa bar.

Valencia, Spain

Check out the coastline and beaches, then explore the shops downtown.

Washington, D.C.

Explore the museums and monuments, including the National Mall and Smithsonian Institution.

San Juan, Puerto Rico

Explore Old San Juan, its cobblestone streets, beaches, and local food scene.

Athens, Greece

Explore the Acropolis and Parthenon, then try traditional Greek restaurants.

New York City

Explore Downtown Manhattan and Central Park, and visit the Museum of Art.

U.S. Virgin Islands

Relax on the beaches, explore the local culture, and try Caribbean cuisine.
